## Our Services

### ➤ Mini Intensives (3–4 Hours)

Deep, focused one-on-one sessions with a trauma-informed therapist. Ideal for trauma processing, transitions, and breakthroughs.

### ➤ Daylong Healing Intensives

Includes a therapy session plus one of the following: yoga therapy, massage therapy, or spiritual advisory session.

### ➤ Outdoor Intensives

Nature-based therapy experiences — conducted in forests, trails, or other outdoor settings. Available as mini or full-day options.

### ➤ 3–4 Day Backpacking Therapy Trips

Immersive therapeutic backpacking in the wilderness. Includes daily individual and group therapy, somatic practices, and deep emotional processing — all in the healing power of nature.

## Our Approach: Science Meets Soul

All intensives are grounded in research-backed trauma modalities:

- Brainspotting (BSP)
- Somatic Experiencing (SE)
- Internal Family Systems (IFS)
- Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT)
